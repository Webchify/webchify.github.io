FAQs

What is Webchify?

A Chrome extension that researches Daraz Pakistan listings: products, prices, sold counts, ratings, sellers, sourcing options and ad signals, so you can judge a product before spending on stock or ads.

Which marketplace does it cover?

Pakistan at launch. Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Myanmar and Nepal are marked Coming Soon.

How do I pay?

Choose a plan in the extension, and you will receive a secure card-payment link. You pay on the payment provider's secure page; Webchify never sees or stores your card details.

How is my plan activated?

After payment, your plan is activated on your account. If activation ever takes longer than expected, contact support with your payment reference and it will be sorted out.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancelling stops future billing. Your paid access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for, then your account drops to the Free plan.

How many devices can I use?

Free: 1 device. Starter: 2 devices. Pro: 4 devices. You can remove an old device to free a slot.

Is the data always accurate?

Product, price, sold, rating and seller data comes from Daraz and third-party sites, which change constantly. Webchify shows what it found at scrape time and labels estimates as estimates. Always verify a supplier, price or fee before ordering stock or spending on ads.
